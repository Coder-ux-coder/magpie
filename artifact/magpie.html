Magpie

Notes that Claude files for you

To dictate, tap the microphone on your keyboard.

Magpie keeps your notes in your Claude account, private to you. Claude files each note with your own Claude usage.

Reminders show here while Magpie is open. For an alert when it's closed, add a reminder to your calendar.

Settings

Claude

Claude model

If your plan can't use the model you pick, Claude answers with the closest one it can.

When should Claude remind you?

Claude reads this with every note, to pick good times and understand who people are.

Categories

Make your own categories, each with a colour. Claude files new notes into the one that fits.

Reminders

Due reminders appear at the top of Magpie while it is open. Magpie can't alert you when it's closed, so add the important ones to Google Calendar or Outlook from the reminder's Calendar button.

Your notes

Export your notes as a Magpie file. You can import it into a self-hosted Magpie, or back into this one.
