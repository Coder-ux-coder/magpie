# Code of Conduct

Magpie follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, welcome people of every background and
level of experience, and focus on what is best for the project and its users. Harassment,
insults and personal attacks are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub](https://github.com/Coder-ux-coder/magpie/security/advisories/new) or by contacting
a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially.
